Privacy Policy

Drive AIFF Player

Drive AIFF Player is a private utility that allows authorized users to open and play AIFF audio files stored in Google Drive.

Google account and Drive data

The application uses Google OAuth to request permission to read files from Google Drive and to integrate with Google Drive.

The application may access Google Drive file identifiers, file names and metadata, and the contents of audio files selected by the user.

How data is used

Drive file data is used only to retrieve and play the selected AIFF audio file in the user’s browser.

Storage

The application does not intentionally store Google Drive file contents on an external server. Audio data is processed in the browser for playback.

Sharing

The application does not sell, rent, or intentionally share Google user data with third parties.

Security

Access to Google Drive is handled through Google OAuth. The application does not receive or store the user’s Google password.

Revoking access

Users may revoke the application’s access at any time through their Google Account security and permissions settings.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when the application or its data handling changes.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the support page.
