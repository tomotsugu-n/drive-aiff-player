Drive AIFF Player

Drive AIFF Player is a private utility for playing AIFF files stored in Google Drive.

Basic troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are signed in to the Google account that has access to the file.
  • Confirm that the selected file is an AIFF file.
  • Reload Google Drive and try opening the file again.
  • If playback fails, revoke and re-authorize the application’s Google Drive access.

Contact

For support, contact the developer at your usual TEINEI contact address.
